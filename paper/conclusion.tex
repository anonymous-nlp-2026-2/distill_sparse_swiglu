\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

At moderate sparsity (30\%), output-distribution alignment is the primary design dimension: signal type may account for most of the PPL gap (upper bound; Appendices~\ref{app:k4_downstream},~\ref{app:constraint}).
A $<$1\% predictor reduces TEAL's PPL by 14.5\% via divergent masks; directional downstream gains but knowledge regression persist across scales.
